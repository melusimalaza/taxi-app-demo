🚖 Smart Taxi Tracking — Prototype

Demo mode · mock data

A working demo of a shared-taxi matching app: passengers request a pickup on a route, the closest available driver gets 90 seconds to accept, and you watch the match happen live on the map. All data is mocked and lives only in this browser.

🧍

Passenger Portal

Pick a route & stop, request a taxi, watch it arrive.
🚕

Driver Portal

Go online, receive requests, accept or decline.
For the best demo: open the Passenger Portal in one window and the Driver Portal in another (side by side, or on two devices/browsers). They sync live via the browser's local storage — no server needed. Requests to any driver you're not actively controlling will simply time out after 90s and auto-forward to the next closest driver, which is a good way to show the "declined / timeout" path without touching anything.
